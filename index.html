Social Studio

Instagram ke liye covers, quote posts, carousels aur reel-videos — photo ya video daalo, text likho, download karo. Koi account ya login nahi chahiye.

💡 Kaise use karein:
1) Koi bhi template kholo  2) Photo/video upload karo  3) Text likho, colors badlo  4) PNG Download dabao (video ke liye Video Record & Download).
Highlight kaise karein: kai templates me words ko *star* ke beech likhne se wo color me ya pill/marker me aate hain.
Video: Chrome me aksar WebM banta hai; Instagram ke liye use MP4 me convert kar lo. iPhone Safari me seedha MP4 milta hai.
Fonts: Google Fonts (open license). Photos/video aapki apni hone chahiye — kisi aur ki photo ya logo bina ijazat use na karein.
